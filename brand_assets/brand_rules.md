# GO DESi — Brand Rules for Banner Generation

> This file is read on every generation and sent to the AI as instructions.
> Edit it in plain English. Be specific — the more precise, the more on-brand
> the output. Think of it as briefing a new designer who has never seen your work.

## Brand Personality
- Indian, warm, joyful, rooted in the "mela" (Indian fair) aesthetic.
- Playful and vibrant, never sterile or corporate.
- Celebrates traditional Indian sweets and snacks with a modern, clean twist.
- "Goodness of Jaggery" is a recurring trust cue — natural, wholesome.

## Colour Palette
- Forest green as a signature background colour.
- Warm terracotta / burnt orange for energy and contrast.
- Cream and off-white for clean space and text legibility.
- Accent yellows and blues drawn from existing pack designs.
- Avoid cold corporate blues, neon, or washed-out pastels.

## Typography & Headlines
- Headlines: bold, rounded, friendly, high legibility. Sentence case or title case.
- Keep headlines SHORT — 3 to 6 words. Example tone: "Sweet Coconut Treats for Ugadi".
- Always include a clear call-to-action button, default text: "Order Now".
- Text must be highly legible against the background — never place small text on busy areas.

## Layout Rules
- Product pack shot(s) must be prominent and appetising — the hero of the banner.
- Decorative festive elements (marigold, leaves, rangoli, torans, coconut pieces,
  nuts) framing the product, but never cluttering or covering it.
- Leave breathing room — do not fill every pixel.
- The GO DESi logo (orange starburst badge) should be present, usually upper area.
- Include the small green veg-mark dot where pack shots are shown, if appropriate.

## Copy Examples (tone reference — match this voice)
- "Sweet Coconut Treats for Ugadi"
- "Goodness of Jaggery"
- "Order Now"
- "Made with love, the desi way"
- "Real ingredients. Real taste."

## Hard Don'ts
- No fake/garbled text or nonsense lettering — if text can't be rendered cleanly, prefer less text.
- No Western/generic dessert styling (no cupcakes, macarons, frosting swirls).
- No stock-photo people unless explicitly requested.
- Do not invent claims, prices, or offers unless the prompt specifies them.
- Do not distort or recolour the actual product pack — keep packaging accurate to the reference.
